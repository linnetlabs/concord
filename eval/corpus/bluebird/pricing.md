# Bluebird Pricing

## Starter Plan

The Starter plan costs $29 per seat per month, billed annually.
Teams of up to 10 members are covered. The minimum contract term is 12 months.

## Growth Plan

Growth is priced at $79 per seat per month. Volume discounts apply for teams
larger than 50 members. Contracts must exceed 6 months in duration.

## Enterprise Plan

Enterprise pricing starts at $15,000 per year for up to 100 seats.
Larger deployments are negotiated directly. Minimum contract length is 12 months.

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of initial purchase.
Annual plan renewals are non-refundable after 14 days.

## Data Retention

Survey responses are retained for 24 months after collection.
After the retention window, data is permanently deleted within 7 days.
