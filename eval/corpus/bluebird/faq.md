# Frequently Asked Questions

## How much does Bluebird cost?

Our most popular plan starts at $29 per seat. See our pricing page for full details.
Enterprise contracts are negotiated individually and typically start at $15,000 per year.

## How long is the free trial?

The trial period is 21 days. No credit card is required to start.

## How are results kept anonymous?

Results are aggregated. We require at least 5 respondents before showing any output.
Individual-level data is never shared with managers or HR.

## How long is my data kept?

Survey data is stored for 24 months. After this window it is deleted within 7 days.
You may request early deletion at any time via the data settings panel.

## What happens if my group is too small?

If fewer than 5 people complete the survey, results are withheld entirely.
You will see a message indicating that the threshold was not met.

## Can I extend my trial?

Trial extensions are available for up to 14 days on request.
