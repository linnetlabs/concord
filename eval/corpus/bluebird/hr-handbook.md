# HR Handbook

## Using Bluebird for Team Surveys

Bluebird is approved for use in annual engagement surveys and pulse checks.
Results should be reviewed at the team level, never at the individual level.

## Anonymity Requirements

Our contract with Bluebird requires that results only be shown to managers when
a minimum of 8 respondents have completed the survey for that team.
Smaller groups are automatically suppressed by the platform.

## Data Governance

Under our data processing agreement, survey data is retained by Bluebird for up to
36 months. HR may request deletion of a specific cohort's data at any time.

## Refund and Contract Terms

Our current contract is annual. The 30-day refund window applies to new seats
added mid-contract. Core contract renewals are handled by procurement.

## Session Security

Employees should be aware that Bluebird sessions time out after 4 hours of
inactivity. This is shorter than the platform default for added security.
