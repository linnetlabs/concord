# Onboarding Guide

## Account Setup

New accounts are provisioned within 1 business day of payment confirmation.
The free trial lasts 14 days and requires no credit card.

## Minimum Group Size

To protect respondent anonymity, results are only shown when at least 5 participants
have completed the survey. Groups below this threshold are suppressed.

## Pricing Reference

The Starter plan is available from $39 per seat per month for small teams.
Growth plan seats are $79 per seat monthly, the same as on our pricing page.

## Data Handling

All survey data is retained for 36 months by default, in line with our data policy.
Customers on the Enterprise plan may request shorter retention windows.

## Trial Extensions

Free trial extensions of up to 7 days may be granted on request.
To qualify, the team must have at least 3 active members during the trial period.
